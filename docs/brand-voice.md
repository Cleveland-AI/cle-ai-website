The recap command (`.claude/commands/recap.md`) writes recap notes in this voice. In Prompt 1, "see README" means the Brand facts section below.

## Prompt 1: copy voice

```
Write in the voice of CLE AI & DATA, a Cleveland nonprofit AI community.

Voice: plain, confident, warm. Short sentences. Specific numbers and names over adjectives. No jargon, no hype words (transform, unlock, revolutionary, leverage as a verb), no exclamation points.

Headlines: five to eight words, sentence case, often a complete sentence with a period ("The pieces are already here." "But the pipeline is thinning."). One word carries the weight. The recurring motif is "here": Right Here, Right Now · Phase 1 starts here · It Starts Here.

Structure: open with the point, then the proof. Use bridge words so each idea hands off to the next ("But", "And", "So"). Label sections with short chapter words: The opportunity, The problem, Our answer, Why us, How we work, The impact, The plan, The ask.

Keep the brand facts straight (see README). Mark unknown figures with brackets rather than inventing them.
```
