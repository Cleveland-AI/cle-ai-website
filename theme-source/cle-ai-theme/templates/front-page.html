Making AI accessible to everyone.

Talks, socials, workshops and hikes for anyone in Cleveland curious about AI, from first-timers to decision makers.

4,284 members · 183 events since 2010 · 4.8 from 1,465 reviews

The big nights

A full room, with a seat for you.

A few times a year we fill an auditorium with talks from people at AWS, OpenAI, Nutanix, Arista Networks and Further. Stay for the hour after and meet the room, whatever brought you.

Once a month

Hot dogs at Happy Dog.

No slides, no agenda, no experience needed. One evening a month, 6 to 8 PM, at 5801 Detroit Avenue in Gordon Square. Say hello to whoever is nearest.

Learning by doing

Learn it by doing it.

Hands-on workshops with partner companies. Bring a laptop and your questions; someone who has done it before is at the whiteboard. The goal: Cleveland's AI and data jobs, filled by Cleveland people.

Outside

Not everything happens indoors.

Hikes on the North Chagrin bridle trail, and volunteer days around town. Twenty-eight of us walked the trail in August. It's the easiest way to meet everyone.

How it started

Sixteen years of showing up.

In 2010 Doug Meil started a Hadoop user group. Cleveland AI formed in 2017, and in 2024 the two merged. 183 events later, there's still room at the table. Come to the next one.
